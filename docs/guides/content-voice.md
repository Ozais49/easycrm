# Content & Voice

> Purpose: how the UI talks. Status: Draft

## Voice
- Friendly, clear, brief. Talk to the user ("you"), not about "the user".
- Plain words over jargon. Client companies are labeled **"Company"** (placeholder — not "Account" or "Client"; revisit)

## Labels
- Sentence case for buttons and headings ("Add contact", not "Add Contact")
- Buttons start with a verb: Save, Add, Send, Approve
- Consistent terms across the app — maintain a glossary below

## Empty states
- Say what goes here + one action: "No deliverables yet. Add the first one."

## Errors
- What happened + what to do: "Couldn't send the message. Check the WhatsApp connection in Settings."
- Never show stack traces or raw codes

## Confirmation
- Destructive actions name the object: "Delete 'Wedding shoot – Sita'?"

## Glossary
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Company (placeholder) | A client organization the tenant works with |
| Deal | A potential sale in the pipeline |
| Deliverable | A piece of work owed to a client |
| Portfolio item | A showcased past project |
| Member | A person in your organization (not "user") |
| Role | A named set of permissions that can be given to members |
| Team | A group of members that records can be shared with |
| Organization | The workspace a team works in (shown in the org switcher) |
